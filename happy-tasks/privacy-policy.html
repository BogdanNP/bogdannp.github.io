Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

Introduction

Happy Tasks ("the App") is a task management application designed with your privacy in mind. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your data.

Data Storage and Collection

🔒 Local-Only Storage

Happy Tasks stores all your data locally on your device. Your data never leaves your device.

This includes:

  • Tasks and subtasks you create
  • Categories you define
  • App preferences and settings (theme, notifications, etc.)

🛡️ No Data Collection

Happy Tasks does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information:

  • We do not collect personal identifiable information
  • We do not track your usage or behavior
  • We do not use analytics services
  • We do not display advertisements
  • We do not transmit any data to external servers

📵 No Account Required

The App works entirely offline and does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use it.

Permissions

Happy Tasks may request the following permissions:

🔔 Notifications (Optional)

If you choose to enable task reminders, the App will request permission to send local notifications. These notifications are scheduled and displayed entirely on your device and do not involve any external servers.

🌐 Internet Access

The App may request internet permission for potential future features. However, in the current version, no data is transmitted over the internet.

Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Your data is protected by your device's security features (screen lock, encryption, etc.)
  • Uninstalling the App will permanently delete all associated data
  • The App does not have access to any data outside of its own storage

Children's Privacy

Happy Tasks does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

🚀 Future Features

In future versions, Happy Tasks may offer optional cloud synchronization features. If such features are added:

  • They will be entirely optional
  • You will be clearly informed before any data is transmitted
  • A separate consent will be required
  • This Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.

Your Consent

By using Happy Tasks, you consent to this Privacy Policy.

This privacy policy applies to the Happy Tasks application.
